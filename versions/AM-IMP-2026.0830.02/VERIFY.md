# Verify

- Run `npm run dryrun:contract-review`.
- Confirm a source PDF with two pages adds two pages to the composite draft.
- Confirm a PNG or JPEG quotation adds one scaled A4 page.
- Open an existing review link and confirm the merged draft appears in the page.
- Open every original attachment with its separate control.
- Confirm all document responses are `no-store` and require the valid review token.
