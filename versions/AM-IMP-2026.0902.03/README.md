# AM-IMP-2026.0902.03 - Structured contract fields, signatures, and identity appendix

Engineering contract versions now preserve their own structured project, party, term, and risk fields. The next-version editor carries those values forward and makes the entry timing explicit: draft fields may remain under discussion, while formal issue readiness requires the project, Party A, schedule, warranty, promissory-note, and delay-penalty fields.

When structured fields exist, the generated contract removes the duplicated Word opening parties, project name, project address, total, payment schedule, and old dates. The legal body begins at Article 3, uses the structured work scope, amount, payment, dates, and acceptance data. Articles 11 and 12 preserve the template's full standard wording and replace only the versioned warranty, performance-bond, amount, and delay-percentage values.

The final output has a dedicated signing section, reuses the contractor's verified electronic signature on the promissory note, keeps the promissory note on one page, and appends the verified contractor identity-card front and back as a private personal-data page. No customer values, identity images, or production credentials are stored in this package.
