# Individual claim form availability

Status: Installed

Managers can disable/enable individual forms while preserving group assignments, immutable versions and claims. HOZO V3 is retired and locked off. HOZO legacy selection uses external_claim_only for the existing legacy validation path, avoiding retired V3 membership calls; other tenants retain their original mode. Selector and legacy LIFF verification recheck availability; cached selected URLs are cleared when disabled. No payments or LINE notifications sent during verification.
